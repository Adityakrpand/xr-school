# Soluble and Insoluble Substances — Simulation Design

## Module definition

- **Slug:** `soluble-insoluble-substances`
- **Curriculum:** Class 5 Science, Chapter 7: Experiments with Water, Activity 3
- **Boards:** CBSE, ICSE, and state boards using the supplied activity
- **Format:** `immersiveVr`
- **Status:** `approved` pending internal and school pilots
- **Evidence confidence:** `expertDesigned`
- **Duration:** 5 minutes; hard stop at 8 minutes
- **Comfort risk:** `low` — stationary viewpoint with optional desktop orbit
- **Performance target:** 72 FPS on Meta Quest using procedural geometry and no external assets

## XR fit justification

The learner conducts six repeatable pour-and-stir trials in a transparent vessel. Particle-scale dissolving, settling sediment, suspended flour, and a floating oil layer make different outcomes spatially comparable from any angle. VR provides every learner with close-up evidence without spills, consumables, cleanup, or restricted visibility around a classroom demonstration.

## Learning objective

Students will test and classify sugar, salt, sand, cooking oil, flour, and small stones as soluble or insoluble in water and explain that a dissolved substance still exists even when it is no longer visible.

## Five-stage flow

1. **Meet the mystery substances:** Inspect all six substances and predict whether each will dissolve or remain separate.
2. **The disappearing experiment:** Add and stir every substance, replaying any trial to compare dissolving, settling, clouding, and floating.
3. **Discover the secret:** Compare soluble and insoluble outcomes and explain that dissolved particles spread throughout the water.
4. **Water Detective challenge:** Sort all six substances into the soluble and insoluble groups with retryable hints.
5. **Mystery solved:** Review the evidence and earn the Junior Water Detective badge.

## Assessment hooks

- Six pre-experiment predictions.
- Six observed pour-and-stir outcomes.
- One concept check distinguishing dissolving from vanishing.
- Six final classifications with corrective hints and retry.

## Safety and accessibility

- Stationary VR camera; no artificial locomotion, flashes, sudden cuts, or forced underwater movement.
- Spoken narration is duplicated in the in-world mission board and browser control panel.
- Large text-labelled controls, high contrast, and `aria-live` feedback.
- Full browser and touch operation when WebXR is unavailable.
