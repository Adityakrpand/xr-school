## XR fit justification

The learner moves through a stationary virtual textile laboratory where an invisible polymer-to-fibre process becomes visible and repeatable. Fibre samples, strength and warmth tests, wrinkle and drying comparisons, blended fabrics, and a safely separated fire hazard can be inspected side by side. VR makes microscopic manufacturing and comparative material properties spatial, memorable, and safe without specialist machinery or exposing students to flame.

## Learning objective

Students will explain how a polymer is converted into fibre, distinguish rayon, nylon, polyester, and acrylic by their properties and uses, identify rayon as semi-synthetic, and apply the rule that synthetic fabrics must be kept away from fire.

## Eight-stage flow

1. **Scan the materials:** Compare cotton, wool, silk, nylon, polyester, and acrylic and identify natural and human-made fibres.
2. **Make a synthetic fibre:** Soften polymer, pass it through a spinneret, stretch the strands, and wind them onto a spool.
3. **Discover rayon:** Transform wood-pulp cellulose into rayon and connect artificial silk with its absorbency and semi-synthetic origin.
4. **Test nylon:** Stretch the fibre, add weights, and connect strength, lightness, and elasticity with common uses.
5. **Explore polyester:** Test wrinkle resistance and quick drying, then create polycot and polywool blends.
6. **Explore acrylic:** Compare weight, warmth, and wool-like products.
7. **Compare and stay safe:** Match all four fibres to their defining properties and activate the keep-away-from-fire barrier.
8. **Unlock the fibre vault:** Match objects and properties to the correct fibres, complete the safety lock, and review the lesson.

## Assessment hooks

- Scan and classify at least three natural and human-made samples.
- Complete the four manufacturing steps in order.
- Complete ordered rayon processing and property observations.
- Perform nylon strength, polyester performance, and acrylic warmth tests.
- Match four fibre properties, four everyday objects, and four defining clues.
- Complete three fire-safety lock steps before opening the vault.

## Safety and accessibility

- Stationary VR camera; no artificial locomotion, flashes, sudden cuts, or forced movement.
- The flame is a stylised visual behind a barrier; learners never handle fire.
- Spoken narration is duplicated in the in-world mission board and browser control panel.
- High-contrast controls, visible progress, retryable feedback, and `aria-live` updates.
- Full mouse, touch, keyboard-focus, and browser operation when WebXR is unavailable.
